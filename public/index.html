scope check
  • Instalment cadence: confirm typical schedule per college (annual, semester, etc.) and how many years.
  • First instalment trigger: when is the first commission known — at enrolment, at start of term, on first tuition receipt?
  • College reporting: which colleges report instalments proactively vs need chasing.
  • Reminder cadence: how often to nudge a college after a missed instalment, and through what channel.
  • Leeds CRM sync: CSV export, direct API, or a webhook on confirmation.
  • Production scope: shared accounts, audit trail, scheduled reminder emails, payment receipts upload.
